# Worked design · a survey sentry

**GENERALIZED example.** A tripod at an excavation tracks a moving survey marker, charges a rangefinder and sends a visible pulse to the first accepted surface. The pose problem is the same as a sentry cannon; the game event here records a measurement.

Place a yaw bearing at local (0,0.85,0), a pitch hinge at (0,0.28,0) in the bearing frame and twin tubes on the pitch frame at x=±0.14. Define a shared exit socket at (0,0,0.9). Solve yaw and elevation from the world hinge to the marker, turn along the shortest arc, and carry the lens housing in the pitch frame. For each sleeve store its center, local +Z slide axis and rest extension.

| Age in the proposed action | Pose and cue |
|---|---|
| Scan | Lower nose slightly, sweep yaw, emit a quiet bearing click on reversal. |
| Lock through tick 7 | Ease into aim and tighten two optic brackets. Sample the exit from the fitted barrel frame. Pitch a short charge upwards. |
| Tick 8 | Freeze socket O and forward F, resolve the allowed distance L, draw shell and inner line from O to O+FL. Record measurement once. |
| Ticks 9–14 | Slide sleeves backward by 0.12 blocks and ease them home. Remove the pulse at its own expiry. |

Keep all model origins at the neutral tripod anchor. Compose sleeve travel before applying pitch. Draw a diagnostic point on each tube mouth and one at the shared socket; their midpoint should coincide at discharge. Check a marker directly overhead, a mid-charge obstruction and interrupted recovery. These timings are example art direction.
