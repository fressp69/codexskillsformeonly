# Keep a visible volume and its contact result aligned

**OBSERVED.** CannonBeam defines a swept square by orthonormal right, up and forward axes. bounds produces a world-aligned broad phase. firstContact considers local axes, global axes and nondegenerate cross products. clearLength walks in short longitudinal segments and tests individual block collision boxes shifted from block-local coordinates to world coordinates. The barrel-to-muzzle path is checked too. TurretMachine.fire collects eligible intersecting players once, then uses one resolved shot length for visuals. The thin centerline guide during charging is an approximation and can extend past cover that clips a shell corner.

**GENERALIZED algorithm.** For a square/rectangle sweeping along F, origin O, transverse half widths w,h and any separating axis n, the face has projected radius w|n·R|+h|n·U|. An axis-aligned obstacle with half extents e contributes |nx|ex+|ny|ey+|nz|ez. With center difference d = n·(C−O), speed v=n·F and summed radius r, solve |d−tv| ≤ r. Intersect the travel intervals from every candidate axis and the permitted range. For v near zero reject disjoint projections immediately. The earliest surviving t is contact. Keep one tolerance convention for grazing contact, zero length and endpoints. A conservative full-face stop can shorten the whole effect when just one corner hits.

**Render contract.** Give rendering an immutable snapshot of origin, orthonormal basis, clipped length, impact points and expiry. For segment [a,b], center = O+F(a+b)/2, scale = (2w,2h,b−a). Preserve roll for square sections. Layer translucent shell, narrower emissive core and sparse transverse sparks. Truncate the final segment to the same length; skip empty intervals. A short visual lifetime does not authorize repeat hits. A detector volume or harmless scanning gate can use the same representation with a different gameplay consumer.

**RECOMMENDED checks.** Test a diagonal slab, fence edge, a target behind the first contact, a nearly vertical shot, and an unloaded boundary. Compare contact on the thick face with a center-only ray. Reject unbounded chunk generation for a visual query. Profile the number of candidate shapes visited before changing segment length.
