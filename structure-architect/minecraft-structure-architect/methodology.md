# Methodology extraction report

Source basis: the SkillsSMP Paper 1.21.11 Java 21 project. The paths below are relative to src/main/java/dev/skillssmp/altar unless stated otherwise. The examples in this skill are new structure plans. No historical design intention is assumed beyond what the code, comments and tests establish.

| Implementation evidence | Reusable decision | Boundary or proposed extension |
|---|---|---|
| AltarGeometry initializes immutable vertices, edges and 16 survey points. Its half-step angle aligns face normals with the mask. | Share continuous geometry between the survey, writer and renderer, then derive physical mount cells once. | Recheck symmetry on the integer lattice after changing the radius. |
| NexusMasonry.design emits a complete floor, walking clearance, four gateway breaks, low perimeter, eight solid multipart pillars and a lodestone marker. Later entries resolve overlapping roles. | Compose architectural solids and reserved voids in a deterministic plan. The floor, foundations, pillars and connecting architecture are permanent world blocks. | Cell connectivity and the approach-to-deck transition still merit a navigation test. Four openings alone do not prove access on every slope. |
| AltarTerrain samples the expanded octagon and separates center feet from deck elevation. AltarSite.Layout validates relative support depths. NexusMasonry.withTerrainSupports scans beneath the floor. | Keep the principal structure level and vary the substructure against local ground. | The 16 samples are not a serialized per-cell foundation plan. Save exact support choices for strict reconstruction after terrain edits. |
| AltarProtection checks footprint overlap with WorldGuard regions and declines known unsupported claim systems. AltarGeneration and AltarSearch gate survey work on chunk availability and bounded budgets. | Choose sites with an explicit permission policy and a resumable search. | Bounding-box overlap is conservative. Larger structures benefit from a region query over the actual build volume. |
| AltarStore and core/SafeFileStore stage durable updates. AltarSite derives a stable ID from world and original coordinates. AltarManager guards owned masonry against block edits, pistons and explosions. | Distinguish site identity, built cells and transient objects. Decide who may alter each. | For movable or versioned structures, persist an independent ID and design revision. |
| NexusModel specifies 35 supernatural display pieces for the surveyed layout. Its separate four-piece fallback serves legacy sites without layout metadata. NexusVisual owns interpolation, orbit phases, local cues and release effects. | Use entity transforms for motion while real blocks provide travel and interaction surfaces. | Fixed anchor-chunk ownership works at monument scale. Partition a larger animated facility. |
| AltarAssemblies ensures one assembly per site. AltarManager drives updates and unload cleanup. Ritual/ConsumptionManager checks the ascent path during actual movement. | Share site coordinates across construction and gameplay while preserving runtime collision checks. | A successful placement survey cannot guarantee a clear route after later edits. |
| Test sources NexusGeometryTest and NexusLifecycleTest exercise several structural and recovery invariants. | Validate geometry and persistence separately from visual review. | These files were inspected during extraction. No Minecraft session or plugin test run was performed for this documentation task. |

The castled roof, directional stair rotation, navigation flood-fill, global time budget, underground cavity survey and pump wheel are generalizations or proposed improvements. They are not presented as previously implemented Nexus features.
